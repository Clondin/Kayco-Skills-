## Bundled Site Assets

`assets/site-assets/` contains a curated GitHub-friendly copy of Beleaves site imagery from:

`C:\Users\clond\Beleaves-Site-\public`

Selection rule: JPG, JPEG, and PNG files up to 1.5 MB each were copied with their relative folders preserved. This provides real Beleaves product images, greenhouse/brand imagery, hangtag art, and category assets without committing the largest source renders.

Use these bundled folders first for most brand outputs:

- `assets/site-assets/products/fresh/` for Beleaves Gardens greens, bowls, basil, and fresh produce.
- `assets/site-assets/products/frozen/` for frozen vegetables and frozen prepared items.
- `assets/site-assets/products/dates/` and `assets/site-assets/products/date-syrup/` for date products.
- `assets/site-assets/products/beans/`, `rice/`, `quinoa/`, and `flour/` for pantry products.
- `assets/site-assets/products/fries/`, `veggie-fries/`, `veggie-blends/`, `veggie-burger/`, and `onion-rings/` for frozen snack and meal products.
- `assets/site-assets/products/fruit/` and `mushrooms/` for produce categories.
- Top-level bundled files such as `greenhouse-interior.png` and `hangtag.png` for brand atmosphere and site-style cues when available.

Large excluded media remains available only from the local site source unless Git LFS or another large-asset storage approach is added.

## Selection Rules

- Prefer exact SKU, slug, or product-family assets over generic produce images.
- Prefer `assets/site-assets/` when it contains the needed product or brand image.
- Prefer packshots for retail/ecommerce and greenhouse/lifestyle imagery for campaign or presentation covers.
- Use local source originals when higher resolution is needed and the file is available.
- Do not move or modify source assets in the Beleaves site folder; copy into the working project when needed.
