# Beleaves Brand Guide

This guide captures usable Beleaves direction for Codex work. Treat actual supplied Beleaves files and the current site source as the source of truth whenever they are available.

## Brand Character

- Natural grocery brand with a fresh, leafy, wholesome personality.
- Broad product world: fresh greenhouse greens, frozen vegetables, dates and date syrup, beans, grains, rice, quinoa, flour, fries, fruit, mushrooms, and veggie products.
- Feels clean, dependable, family-friendly, and produce-forward.
- The Beleaves Gardens sub-line can feel especially fresh, vertical-farm, and greenhouse-grown.

## Visual Signals

- Logo: brown script wordmark with a green leaf and water droplets.
- Natural leaf/greenhouse cues should feel crisp and real, not decorative or cartoonish unless using existing bundled cartoon product assets.
- Use packshots prominently for retail and ecommerce.
- Use hang-tag shapes and package-forward layouts when echoing the site style.
- Fresh greens work can use controlled-environment greenhouse imagery and clean bright surfaces.

Avoid making the entire piece a single green wash. Use white, natural backgrounds, product color, and category browns to keep the brand appetizing and easy to scan.

## Typography

- Site direction uses `Plus Jakarta Sans` for headline, body, and label roles.
- Good fallbacks: `Aptos`, `Inter`, `Arial`, `Calibri`.
- Logo should stay as the supplied image; do not imitate the script wordmark in live text.
- Use strong, clean sans hierarchy for sell sheets and ecommerce.

## Layout

- For ecommerce and sell sheets: packshot first, then product name, size, SKU if provided, key facts, and clear buyer-readable hierarchy.
- For fresh greens: bright clean layouts, greenhouse/freshness imagery, leafy accents, and generous white or pale green space.
- For dates/date syrup: warmer brown and golden accents with natural sweetener language.
- For beans, grains, rice, and quinoa: warm pantry browns with clean ingredient or bowl cues.
- For frozen vegetables and veggie products: crisp cold/fresh cues, product packshot, and simple preparation benefit language.
- Use rounded tag shapes sparingly when referencing the site hang-tag style.

## Product Families

Common product-family starts visible in the site asset structure:

- `fresh`: baby romaine, crispy leaf, spring mix, basil, bowls, mixed greens
- `frozen`: frozen vegetables, mozzarella sticks, meals, pizza, gnocchi, ravioli
- `dates` and `date-syrup`: Medjool, Deglet Noor, frozen dates, date syrup
- `beans`, `rice`, `quinoa`, and `flour`
- `fries`, `veggie-fries`, `veggie-blends`, `veggie-burger`, and `onion-rings`
- `fruit` and `mushrooms`

Match color and copy to the category. Greens should feel fresh and crisp; dates should feel naturally sweet; pantry items should feel reliable and staple-oriented.

## Copy Guidance

- Good phrasing territory: fresh you can trust, greenhouse grown, ready to enjoy, naturally sweet, pantry staples, wholesome convenience, family-friendly meals.
- Use site-supported phrases carefully for Beleaves Gardens, such as pesticide-free, no bugs, no washing needed, vertically grown, greenhouse grown, and Grown by 80 Acres Farms, only when the request is about the matching fresh greens product line or the user confirms the claim.
- For date products, site copy supports natural sweetness, Medjool and Deglet Noor language, date syrup, and simple ingredient positioning, but verify specific claims before production.
- Avoid unsupported organic, non-GMO, kosher, gluten-free, allergen, sustainability, water-use, or nutrition claims unless visible in supplied assets or confirmed by the user.

## Accessibility and Production

- Maintain strong contrast on pale green and photography.
- Keep editable text out of generated images whenever possible.
- Leave space for hechsher, legal, nutrition, and retailer compliance elements in packaging/ecommerce concepts.
- Flag anything that needs regulatory, kosher, or packaging review before production.
