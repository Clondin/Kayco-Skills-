# Beleaves Brand

Use this skill for Beleaves-branded creative work across presentations, sell sheets, ecommerce content, social assets, website graphics, POS, packaging concepts, and brand identity explorations.

## Core Workflow

1. Read `references/brand-guide.md` before making visual or copy decisions.
2. Read `references/asset-map.md` before choosing product, site, or category source assets.
3. Use bundled assets before falling back to external source folders:
   - Logo: `assets/beleaves-logo.png`
   - Lightweight site product and brand imagery: `assets/site-assets/`
4. Preserve the brand's natural grocery cues:
   - leafy script wordmark
   - deep green and fresh greenhouse palette
   - clean produce photography
   - hang-tag and package-forward retail language
   - wholesome pantry, frozen, fresh, and date-product credibility
5. Use actual product shots, packaging, and source artwork whenever available. Do not fabricate product packaging, labels, claims, certifications, UPCs, hechsher marks, or nutrition facts.
6. Keep outputs practical for KAYCO use: editable where possible, easy to hand off, and grounded in real Beleaves product categories.

## Asset Rules

- Do not recreate, redraw, recolor, or generate the Beleaves logo with imagegen.
- Search bundled site assets before using generic imagery:
  `assets/site-assets/`
- Search the local source root when bundled assets do not cover the need:
  `C:\Users\clond\Beleaves-Site-\public`
- Prefer product-specific source folders over generic produce imagery when the user names a SKU, category, retailer asset, or sell sheet.
- Keep brand assets in their original aspect ratio and avoid stretching, clipping, or placing the logo over busy food photography.
- Do not commit or embed oversized source imagery unless the user explicitly asks for a large-media workflow.

## Imagegen Rules

Use imagegen only for supporting visuals that do not require exact product or label accuracy:

- leafy greenhouse atmosphere
- fresh produce, water droplets, harvest, pantry, or clean kitchen scenes
- text-free hero plates with negative space for editable copy
- concept moodboards or campaign backgrounds

Do not ask imagegen for:

- Beleaves logos or wordmarks
- readable packaging labels
- kosher marks, nutrition panels, UPCs, claims, or certifications
- exact product packshots
- retailer-ready ads with baked-in text

## Copy Tone

- Fresh, wholesome, clean, and practical.
- Product-forward rather than hype-driven.
- Strong for grocery buyers, ecommerce, and family food contexts.
- For Beleaves Gardens greens, use greenhouse/freshness language only when supported by the source asset or site copy.
- Avoid invented sourcing details, certifications, dates, pricing, retailer claims, sustainability metrics, and performance claims.

## Validation

Before final delivery:

- Check that the logo is readable and not distorted.
- Check that claims are supported by supplied assets, site copy, or user-provided facts.
- For PowerPoint or document outputs, keep important text editable.
- For packaging or ecommerce work, flag any placeholder product facts that need brand, kosher, or regulatory review.
