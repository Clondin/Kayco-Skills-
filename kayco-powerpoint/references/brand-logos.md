## Presentation Use

- Prefer the KAYCO logo as the main deck brand mark.
- Use individual brand logos only when the slide is specifically about that brand, brand portfolio, product examples, supplier/retailer context, or cross-brand comparison.
- Keep logos on white, pale lavender, or very light neutral backgrounds unless the logo is known to work on dark backgrounds.
- Size brand logos by visual weight, not by identical bounding boxes; some marks are much wider or taller than others.
- Leave clear space around each logo. Avoid putting logos inside busy generated images.
- If a brand slide needs a logo not listed here, use an official source or ask the user for the asset before substituting.
