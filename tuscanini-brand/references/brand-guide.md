# Tuscanini Brand Guide

This guide captures usable Tuscanini direction for Codex work. Treat actual supplied Tuscanini files as the source of truth whenever they are available.

## Brand Character

- Premium Italian kosher pantry brand.
- Classic, elegant, food-first, and approachable.
- Emphasize Italian provenance, craft, appetite appeal, and reliable retail clarity.
- The brand should feel more like a specialty Italian grocer than a corporate packaged-goods system.

## Visual Signals

- Tuscanini wordmark: classic serif, black on light backgrounds.
- Logo architecture: ornamental top flourish, Italian green/red ribbon, "Made in Italy" signal, strong horizontal framing rules.
- Italian cues should be restrained and credible: ribbon accents, produce, table settings, stone, parchment, olive branches, tomatoes, wheat, and simple region-inspired textures.
- Use generous white or warm-light space around the logo.

## Palette

Use this as a working palette when no formal brand guide is supplied:

| Role | Color | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tuscanini black | `#171717` | Wordmark, primary text, refined dividers |
| Italian green | `#007A3D` | Accent ribbon, freshness, olive oil, herbs |
| Italian red | `#C8102E` | Accent ribbon, tomato, sale/feature moments |
| Warm cream | `#FAF4E8` | Backgrounds, editorial panels |
| Parchment | `#E8D8B7` | Subtle texture, recipe cards, POS warmth |
| Tomato red | `#B53624` | Food accents and tomato categories |
| Olive green | `#5F6F31` | Oil, olives, savory pantry cues |
| Balsamic brown | `#4A2B1D` | Vinegar, glaze, premium depth |
| Wheat gold | `#C89A3C` | Pasta, bread, biscotti, warmth |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Product clarity, ecommerce, sell sheets |

Do not let the design become only red/green. The Italian accents should support a warm food palette, not dominate it.

## Typography

- Display: use an elegant serif that respects the logo, such as `Cormorant Garamond`, `Bodoni 72`, `Playfair Display`, `Georgia`, or `Times New Roman` fallback.
- Body/UI: use a clean sans such as `Lato`, `Aptos`, `Inter`, `Arial`, or `Calibri`.
- For sell sheets and retail assets, prioritize legibility over ornament.
- Avoid novelty Italian fonts, distressed faux-rustic type, and script fonts unless directly supported by supplied packaging.

## Layout

- Use product photography prominently; Tuscanini creative should make the product or food outcome easy to understand quickly.
- Favor editorial food layouts: large product/ingredient image, refined headline, clear product family or SKU information, restrained accent rules.
- For sell sheets: prioritize packshot, SKU/product name, key facts, case/size details, and clean buyer-readable hierarchy.
- For presentations: use full-width Tuscanini headers, generous food imagery, and simple refined dividers rather than dense decorative frames.
- For ecommerce: keep image backgrounds clean and product-forward; avoid lifestyle scenes when a marketplace requires packshot clarity.

## Product Families

Common Tuscanini category cues visible in the asset structure:

- olive oil, olives, and vinegars
- tomato products, sauces, paste, and passata
- pasta, gnocchi, pizza, breads, crackers, and biscotti
- tuna and seafood pantry items
- gelato and frozen Italian foods
- beverages, sparkling juice, cola, lemonade, and water
- salts, spices, truffle, peppers, jams, pesto, and condiments

Match color and ingredient cues to the category. Tomato work can lean red and basil green; olive oil can lean olive green, gold, and cream; pasta/bread can lean wheat and parchment; vinegar can lean dark brown and cream.

## Copy Guidance

- Good phrasing territory: authentic Italian taste, pantry staples, crafted for everyday meals, imported Italian flavor, simple serving inspiration.
- Keep claims factual. Only use "Made in Italy", "imported", "kosher", organic, gluten-free, dairy, pareve, non-GMO, or retailer-specific claims when visible in supplied packaging or confirmed by the user.
- Avoid exaggerated romance, fake Italian phrases, and unsupported regional origin claims.
- Prefer short headlines and appetite-forward subheads.

## Accessibility and Production

- Maintain strong contrast on cream, parchment, and photography.
- Keep editable text out of generated images whenever possible.
- Leave space for hechsher, legal, nutrition, and retailer compliance elements in packaging/ecommerce concepts.
- Flag anything that needs regulatory, kosher, or packaging review before production.
