# Tuscanini Brand

Use this skill for Tuscanini-branded creative work across presentations, sell sheets, ads, ecommerce content, packaging concepts, product launch materials, and brand identity explorations.

## Core Workflow

1. Read `references/brand-guide.md` before making visual or copy decisions.
2. Read `references/asset-map.md` before choosing product, packaging, advertising, or POS source assets.
3. Use bundled assets before falling back to external source folders:
   - Logo: `assets/tuscanini-logo.jpg`
   - Lightweight site product/ad imagery: `assets/site-assets/`
4. Preserve the brand's premium Italian food cues:
   - classic Tuscanini wordmark
   - Made in Italy signal
   - Italian green/red accenting
   - warm editorial food styling
   - clean kosher grocery credibility
5. Use actual product shots, packaging, and source artwork whenever available. Do not fabricate product packaging, labels, claims, certifications, UPCs, hechsher marks, or nutrition facts.
6. Keep outputs practical for KAYCO use: editable where possible, easy to hand off, and grounded in real Tuscanini product categories.

## Asset Rules

- Do not recreate, redraw, recolor, or generate the Tuscanini logo with imagegen.
- Search bundled site assets before using generic imagery:
  `assets/site-assets/`
- Search the local source roots when bundled assets do not cover the need:
  `C:\Users\clond\Tuscanini-Site-\public\assets`
  `C:\Users\clond\Kayco\kentmark - Tuscanini`
- If the local asset folders are not synced or files are missing, say so and continue with the bundled logo plus clearly labeled placeholders.
- Prefer product-specific source folders over broad lifestyle imagery when the user names a SKU, category, retailer asset, or sell sheet.
- Keep brand assets in their original aspect ratio and avoid stretching, clipping, or placing the logo over busy food photography.
- Do not commit or embed large 360 GIFs or MP4 video assets unless the user explicitly asks for a large-media workflow.

## Imagegen Rules

Use imagegen only for supporting visuals that do not require exact product/label accuracy:

- Italian ingredient backgrounds
- rustic market, vineyard, olive oil, tomato, pasta, bread, or gelato atmosphere
- text-free hero plates with negative space for editable copy
- concept moodboards or campaign backgrounds

Do not ask imagegen for:

- Tuscanini logos or wordmarks
- readable packaging labels
- kosher marks, nutrition panels, UPCs, claims, or certifications
- exact product packshots
- retailer-ready ads with baked-in text

## Copy Tone

- Premium, warm, appetizing, and concise.
- Italian provenance without overclaiming.
- Product-forward rather than hype-driven.
- Suitable for kosher specialty grocery and mainstream retail contexts.
- Avoid invented awards, sourcing details, certifications, dates, pricing, and performance claims.

## Validation

Before final delivery:

- Check that the logo is readable and not distorted.
- Check that claims are supported by supplied assets or user-provided facts.
- For PowerPoint or document outputs, keep important text editable.
- For packaging or ecommerce work, flag any placeholder product facts that need brand or regulatory review.
